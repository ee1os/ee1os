## Notes

- Duress and reboot PIN entries show only in B (B is the alternates authority,
  `get-alternates-authority` = 10), as doc 04 says. Looking for them in A is a wrong
  expectation, not a bug.
- F6 from B: a matching guess is a *correct* check of A, so A's failure count goes to 0
  (it was 2), not +1 as the checklist line said.
- After the reboot USB data stays off until a space is unlocked (GrapheneOS USB-C port
  default), so nothing is reachable over adb before the first unlock.
- Over adb, `input text` doesn't reach A's PIN field when the lock screen was reached by
  leaving B; tapping the keypad works. Test-harness artefact, not a phone bug.
