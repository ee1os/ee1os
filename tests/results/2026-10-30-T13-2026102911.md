## Unexpected

- **adb dropped when leaving B with the power button (2.1).** Right after the press the phone was on user 0's lock screen, then disappeared from `adb devices` and from the Mac's USB device list until the owner unlocked A. Locking inside A never dropped adb (steps 1, 3, 4), and locking in B after the reboot-PIN boot (5.3) didn't either. Likely the USB-C port policy denying the new USB session that the switch from B to A starts while A is locked; not confirmed. The driver's FAILs after 10:22:14 in the first log part are the script running on without a phone, not results.
- **`PIN entry matched user 10, switching` not found.** A grep of `logcat -d` after 5.2 found neither it nor `StartUnlock`; T12 saw both. The switch itself worked. Either the log line went with the reverted keyguard fixes or the grep came too late for the buffer; to check.
- Driver: auto-confirm left entries in the field at times (T12 note); the driver sends a separate Enter tap whenever the pad is still up 2 s after the digits.
