## Unexpected

- **No feedback when B's check is throttled (step 8.1).** After the reboot, `222222` auto-confirmed at 16:29:30. A was checked (`weaver read failed (INCORRECT_KEY), slot: 2`, throttle 0 ms), then `Verifying primary lockscreen credential for user 10` produced no result line and no Weaver read. Two more tries at 16:29:33 and 16:29:43 did the same. The pad showed nothing: no "Wrong PIN", no countdown, so it looked dead. At 16:32:06 the same PIN worked. Likely cause: B's Weaver slot was in a timeout. B had no successful unlock since step 5, and step 7's wrong PINs, A's correct PINs and the reboot PIN all count against B. B is throttled correctly, but the bouncer should say so.
- **No countdown during a lockout (step 7).** After the 5th failure the message goes back to "Enter PIN"; stock shows "Try again in N seconds". Entries during the lockout are taken and cleared silently.
- **Auto-confirm turns off at ≥5 failures** (AOSP `MIN_FAILED_PIN_ATTEMPTS = 5`) and the Enter key appears. Anyone used to auto-confirm will type the PIN and wait.
- **`init` SIGABRT on every boot**, seen on 2026101516, 2026101520 and 2026102109: `Fatal signal 6 (SIGABRT), code -1 (SI_QUEUE) in tid ~950–1060 (init)` + `crash_dump helper failed to exec, or was killed`, about 3 s into boot. Low PID, sent by another process. Not new in this build; source not identified.
- **USB data is off before the first unlock too.** After each reboot the phone was not on USB at all until unlocked by hand. So any first-unlock check after a reboot needs the owner, not adb.
- `SYSTEM_UPDATE_SETTINGS` / `app.seamlessupdate.client/.Settings` can't be started from `adb shell` (needs `app.seamlessupdate.client.OPEN_SETTINGS`); opened through Settings instead.
